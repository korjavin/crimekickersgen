from `../crimekickerslor`, but framed head-to-toe with an added BODY section that

last word of the NEGATIVES paragraph and paste.

## Notes

- The CHARACTER paragraph is still verbatim canon from the bible — unchanged from
  `avatars.md` so the portraits and the full-body sheets describe the same person.
  The BODY paragraph is new and authored here; it adds build/posture detail and
  contradicts nothing in canon.
- Heights are stated per character but a solo full-body shot carries no scale
  reference, so relative height between the four will not read. If you need the size
  relationship (Pho-boman 140–150 cm vs. the three at 175–180 cm), generate a lineup
  in one frame rather than relying on four solo images.
- Each BODY paragraph ends with stance/posture on purpose — it is the last thing the
  model reads before framing, and it is what keeps the four from all standing the same.
