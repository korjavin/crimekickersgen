# Character avatar prompts — the four Crime Kickers

Reference/avatar portraits for visual consistency. Source of truth:
`../crimekickerslor` — Visual Style Guide (art-style anchor v1) + each hero's
**Appearance (detailed)** block, reused **verbatim** (that verbatim reuse is the
consistency lever — do not reword between characters or between regenerations).

Each prompt below is self-contained: art-style anchor → character block → avatar
framing → negatives. Paste as-is.

**Shared avatar framing** (identical across all four on purpose):
single-character reference portrait, front-facing, centered, waist-up (full-body where
noted), neutral eye-level camera, flat solid single-color backdrop, even soft lighting,
no scene, no background objects, no other characters, no text.

## CK-001 — Windman

> Modern Western graphic-novel comic-book illustration, single comic-page panel.
> Clean confident hand-inked black linework with medium-weight uniform outlines and
> selective heavier contour lines, light cross-hatching for shadow. Flat cel-style
> coloring with subtle cell-shading gradients, limited halftone texture, restrained
> screen-tone dot shading in shadows. Slightly stylized semi-realistic proportions,
> expressive readable faces, deadpan comedic timing. Crisp print-quality finish,
> clear silhouette-first staging, strong readable composition, no photorealism, no
> 3D render look. Muted desaturated naturalistic palette as the project baseline.
>
> CHARACTER: a humanoid man, male, 180 cm tall, moderately stout with a slightly loose but
> stable physique, normal proportions, not heavily muscled; short natural-brown
> chestnut hair; a calm, soft-featured slightly-rounded face with no beard or
> mustache. Across his eyes, a thick cloth EYE BANDAGE that completely covers both
> eyes — top portion blue, lower portion (above the eyebrows) red — fastened near the
> ears, fitting tightly to the face, leaving NO visible eyes. He wears a fitted blue
> T-shirt (not tight), sleeveless or short sleeves to mid-shoulder, bearing a single
> large white letter "W" monogram on the chest. He wears brown knee-length straight-cut
> slightly-baggy shorts, and fully-black flat-soled snug boots with no decoration.
>
> PROP: a small handheld folding fan held in his RIGHT hand, brown wood-textured handle about
> 25 cm long, three rounded slightly-transparent blades about 10 cm each colored green,
> blue, and red, evenly spaced around a central hub — held up at chest height, clearly legible.
>
> FRAMING: single-character reference avatar portrait, front-facing, centered, waist-up,
> neutral eye-level camera, calm neutral expression, arms relaxed. Flat solid
> desaturated slate-BLUE backdrop (his signature color), even soft lighting, no scene,
> no background objects, no other character, no text.
>
> NEGATIVES: no visible eyes and no eye holes in the bandage, no two-letter "WM" monogram
> (single "W" only), no cape, no mask, no helmet, no beret, no purple ninja suit, no cyan
> visor, no katanas, no diving helmet, no soup, no second character, no team logo, no
> manga or anime style, no chibi, no 3D render, no photorealism, no modern smartphones,
> no anachronistic gadgets, no extra fingers, no malformed hands, no extra limbs, no text
> or lettering of any kind, no background scenery.

> clear silhouette-first staging, strong readable composition, no photorealism, no
> 3D render look. Muted desaturated naturalistic palette as the project baseline.
>
> CHARACTER: a small round spoon-shaped being, the smallest hero at roughly 140–150 cm tall,
> with a large round belly, short legs nearly hidden under the belly's curve, and
> short but noticeably muscular arms; his head is shaped like a deep-sea diving
> helmet — a wide metal dome with a wide transparent front visor; the helmet is
> filled with reddish-orange pho broth (with bright-yellow highlights, a warm glow)
> that visibly sloshes and bubbles; his face forms inside the helmet out of the
> soup's ingredients — eyes, eyebrows and cheekbones made of slightly twisted
> noodles, and a nose made of a bright-green sprig of cilantro poking above the
> broth; he has NO separate mouth — the open visor simply shows the soup, and the
> visor IS the face; he wears short black pants (mostly hidden under the belly) and
> fully black, flat, minimalist boots.
>
> EMBLEM: across the full curve of his large round belly, fully visible, rendered at FULL
> CLARITY and FULL SATURATION: a large red Chinese dragon on a blue background, scales and
> claws detailed, tail wrapping around the belly.
>
> FRAMING: single-character reference avatar portrait, front-facing, centered, FULL BODY
> (the complete spoon-shaped silhouette must read), neutral eye-level camera, calm
> neutral noodle-brow expression, arms relaxed at his sides. Flat solid desaturated
> warm-grey backdrop, even soft lighting — the broth and the red-dragon-on-blue emblem
> are the only saturated elements. No scene, no background objects, no other character,
> no text.
>
> NEGATIVES: no separate mouth (the open visor IS the face — do not draw a mouth), no human
> eyes and no blindfold (his eyes are curled noodles), the belly dragon must NOT be hidden,
> cropped, blurred, desaturated or obscured, no chibi or cute-mascot proportions, no cape,
> no folding fan, no eye bandage, no purple ninja suit, no cyan visor, no katanas, no French
> beret, no second character, no team logo, no manga or anime style, no 3D render, no
> photorealism, no modern smartphones, no anachronistic gadgets, no extra fingers, no
> malformed hands, no extra limbs, no text or lettering of any kind, no background scenery.
